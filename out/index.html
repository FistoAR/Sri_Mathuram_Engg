HOW WE BUILD QUALITY

Precision Manufacturing at Every Step

Every product is manufactured through a carefully controlled process using advanced machinery, skilled craftsmanship, and rigorous quality standards. From concept to delivery, we ensure every piece meets the expectations of modern healthcare facilities.

STEP-01

Product Design

Every product begins with a functional design focused on safety, durability, and healthcare requirements.

Product Design

Every product begins with a functional design focused on safety, durability, and healthcare requirements.

STEP-02

Precision Fabrication

High-grade stainless steel and mild steel are laser-cut and welded for structural rigidity.

STEP-03

Powder Coating

Multi-stage pre-treatment and anti-bacterial powder coating for long-lasting corrosion resistance.

STEP-04

Assembly & Fitting

Expert assembly of hydraulic mechanisms, castors, and ergonomic components by trained engineers.

Quality Inspection

Rigorous load testing, safety checks, and ISO compliance verification before final approval.

STEP-06

Packaging & Delivery

Protective multi-layer packaging and dedicated logistics setup for safe on-time dispatch.
